RETAIL

Retail Businesses

Software Solution: Touchscreen POS software with barcode scanning, instant multi-counter inventory deduction, and customer loyalty management.

E-COMMERCE

Digital Commerce

Challenges: Platform transaction commissions, rigid off-the-shelf checkout templates, and lack of B2B wholesale tiered pricing rules.

Software Solution: Custom digital storefronts, faceted search engines, single-page checkouts, and real-time ERP warehouse synchronization.

Explore E-commerce Software →
HEALTHCARE

Clinics & Healthcare

Challenges: Patient appointment scheduling conflicts, lost paper consultation notes, and delayed laboratory test record tracking.

Software Solution: Doctor consultation portals, appointment booking cadences, role-based medical record repositories, and digital billing.

Explore Healthcare Software →
EDUCATION

Education & Academies

Challenges: Disjointed student fee records, manual attendance logs, and chaotic distribution of course materials across email threads.

Software Solution: Student information systems (SIS), automated fee collection reminders, attendance logging, and digital learning portals.

Explore Education Software →
REAL ESTATE

Real Estate & Property

Challenges: Tracking lease expiration dates, tenant maintenance requests lost in WhatsApp messages, and multi-property accounting errors.

Software Solution: Central property management portals, automated lease renewal notices, maintenance ticketing boards, and tenant billing.

Explore Property Software →
LOGISTICS

Logistics & Supply Chain

Challenges: Disorganized dispatch sheets, inability to verify delivery status in real-time, and lost physical delivery receipts.

Software Solution: Dispatch scheduling dashboards, mobile driver apps with digital signature capture, and shipment milestone tracking.

Explore Logistics Software →
MANUFACTURING

Manufacturing & Assembly

Challenges: Raw material stockouts halting factory lines, lack of multi-stage production batch tracking, and poor machine downtime logging.

Explore Manufacturing Software →
HOSPITALITY

Explore Hospitality Software →
FINANCE

Financial & Accounting

Challenges: Time-consuming manual invoice reconciliation, compliance tracking oversights, and human errors in credit checks.

Explore Financial Software →
SERVICES

Professional Services

STARTUPS

Technology Startups
