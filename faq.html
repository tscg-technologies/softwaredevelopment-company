FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Clear, candid answers regarding our custom software engineering, architectures, processes, and support models.

Custom software development is the process of designing, architecting, building, and maintaining software applications tailored specifically to the unique business workflows, rules, and operational requirements of an organization, rather than relying on generic off-the-shelf software packages.

A business should choose custom software when commercial off-the-shelf tools require constant manual workarounds, fail to support core operational processes, impose expensive per-user monthly seat costs as team sizes grow, or fail to integrate cleanly with proprietary internal systems.

Yes. We build custom CRM platforms designed around your specific sales qualification stages, automated follow-up cadences, contact histories, quote generation, and rep commission calculations, eliminating the clutter of generic CRM platforms.

Yes. We engineer unified ERP systems that connect purchasing, multi-location inventory, sales orders, accounts receivable, and vendor payables into a single centralized database with real-time audit logging.

Yes. We engineer touchscreen-friendly retail and restaurant POS systems featuring fast counter checkouts, barcode scanning support, tax-compliant invoice generation, and real-time inventory deduction.

Yes. We engineer complete SaaS foundations including multi-tenant database isolation, subscription plan quotas, role-based access control, tenant administration panels, and self-service customer analytics.

Yes. We build native iOS (Swift), native Android (Kotlin), and cross-platform (Flutter/React Native) mobile applications designed for workforce dispatch, inventory scanning, customer portals, and executive telemetry.

Yes. We build custom API connectors and webhook ingestion listeners to bridge internal software with external payment gateways, courier shipping APIs, SMS providers, CRM systems, and accounting tools.

We follow a disciplined 7-step lifecycle: (1) Requirement Discovery, (2) Scope & Planning, (3) UI/UX Design, (4) Software Architecture, (5) Agile Development Sprints, (6) QA Testing, and (7) Deployment & Ongoing Support.

Estimates depend entirely on functional complexity, data models, integration requirements, user concurrency, and security tiers. Rather than giving arbitrary guesses, we conduct a structured Discovery phase to establish documented scopes before providing milestone estimates.
